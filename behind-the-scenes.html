The Story Behind the Story

The story you just read did not start as a website. It started as a long text file where I wrote down my memories, experiences, and thoughts about my journey through technology.

Before turning it into the version you see today, I wrote the original draft myself, without AI assistance.

I later used ChatGPT as a tool to help me organize, edit, translate, and structure the story. I wanted to document that process as well, so I decided to keep the original draft and the prompts I used.

01 — The Original Draft

This is the original version of my story, preserved as I wrote it. It is not a polished version, and I have intentionally left it untouched.

There may be awkward sentences, repetitions, spelling mistakes, or ideas that were later changed. That is part of the point.

This is where the story actually started.
