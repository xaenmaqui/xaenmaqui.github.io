My Journey Through Technology

TL;DR

I started working with computers when I was a kid, mostly by taking things apart, fixing them, and reinstalling them. Over the years, that turned into building game servers, learning Linux and networking, experimenting with virtualization, and eventually building my own homelab.

I tried programming too, but eventually realized that I was much more interested in the systems underneath the applications.

Today, I'm focusing on IT infrastructure, Linux, and systems administration, learning by building things and documenting what I break along the way.
